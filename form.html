Submission Requirements

Please review these carefully before submitting your design.

For Clubs
  • Must work on the project for at least 1 hour
  • No Hackatime recording required
  • Must upload STL file
  • Must be a part of Hack Club Slack and joined the channel #charmcraft-ysws
Submit Club Form
For Individuals
  • Must work on the project for at least 1 hour
  • No Hackatime recording required
  • Must upload STL file
  • Must be between 13–18 years old (inclusive)
  • Identity must be verified using Hack Club Auth
  • Must be a part of Hack Club Slack and joined the channel #charmcraft-ysws

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about CharmCraft.

What do I need to participate?
Just a Hack Club account, access to the Hack Club Slack, and a design made in beginner-friendly design software. No prior experience needed.
Is CharmCraft free?
Yes! If your design is approved, you get a free 3D-printed charm shipped to you, along with an earring kit.
What file type should I upload?
Upload your design as an STL file so it can be printed. Make sure it meets the submission requirements above.
Who can join?
Girls aged 13–18 from anywhere in the world are welcome. Identity must be verified using Hack Club Auth.
