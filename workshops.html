CharmCraft is a Hack Club YSWS where you design a charm and get an earring kit so you can turn their creations into wearable jewelry. No prior design experience is needed—it works for complete beginners, and girls from anywhere in the world are welcome!


Host a CharmCraft workshop at your club meeting. Members will design their own charm using beginner-friendly design software (following our workshop guide), submit their design, and have it reviewed. Each approved submission earns them a free 3D-printed charm, shipped alongside an earring kit so they can assemble their own personalized earrings at home.
Our workshop guide walks you through the entire process, making it easy to run—even if no one has experience with 3D design.
This workshop is designed to introduce girls to 3D design, creativity, and digital fabrication in a fun, hands-on way while giving them something they can actually wear and keep.
