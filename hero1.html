CharmCraft is a You Ship, We Ship program by Hack Club, a nonprofit network inspiring young people to code.

Design your own custom charm using 3D CAD, submit your project, and we'll manufacture and ship it right to your door, completely free.

Whether you're new to CAD or someone with experience, this program gives you the tools, tutorials, and community to create something real. No experience required. Just curiosity.
